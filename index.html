Submit flags. Track first blood. Climb the board.

This is where DFIR submits flags and writeups for challenges that are already released. Every correct flag is validated server-side — nobody, including this page, ever sees the answer key.

Submit a flag → View leaderboard

1. Submit the flag

Send your flag from the Submit Flag page. Correct = 2 points instantly, no review needed. Wrong flags are rejected instantly — nothing is saved.

2. Submit the writeup

Once you've solved it, attach a writeup any time from the Submit Writeup page — independent of the flag submission.

3. Get reviewed

Admins score writeups 0 (Poor) to 2 (Excellent). Flag (2 pts) + writeup score = your total. First solver gets 🔥 First Blood.
